Making power systems safer

SDG 7 + 9 · Published research

When solar systems fail, detect it earlier

Co-authored published research using PyramidNet and GRU models to diagnose photovoltaic faults. The paper reports 96% accuracy for binary classification and 91% across 12 classes under its evaluation conditions.

03 / The uncomfortable questions

What these experiences changed

I used to think that building something impressive was the hardest part. It isn't. Getting close enough to a problem to understand who actually needs help, testing the solution with them and making it sustainable: that's the work I'm trying to get better at.

Research taught me to question results. Entrepreneurship taught me to question assumptions. Writing taught me that the human side of a problem can't be reduced to a metric.

04 / What's next

Less “innovation” as a slogan. More evidence that it helped.

I'm interested in partnerships around sustainable infrastructure, accessible professional tools, open technical education and community-led problem solving. My goal is to connect rigorous engineering with the people who would actually live with the outcomes.

If you're building in those areas, I'd love to compare notes, collaborate on a pilot or help evaluate what works and what doesn't.
